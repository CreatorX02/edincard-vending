The price of an item is the price displayed on its slot label at the time of purchase, in pounds sterling, inclusive of VAT where applicable.

If the price charged differs from the price on the slot label, the slot label price applies and we'll refund the difference on request.

5. When the contract is made

A contract is formed when your payment is authorised and the machine dispenses your item. If payment is authorised but the machine fails to dispense, no contract is completed and you're entitled to a replacement or a refund under section 6.

6. Failed vends, faults and refunds

If you pay and the item doesn't dispense, or the item dispensed isn't what the slot label described, or arrives with a damaged wrapper:

  • Email info@edincardvending.com within 48 hours, telling us the venue, the slot code and roughly what time it happened. A photo helps.
  • We'll check your report against the machine's vend log.
  • Where the report is supported, we'll offer a replacement item held for you at the venue, or a refund to the card you paid with — your choice.

Refunds are made to the original payment card and typically reach you within 5–10 working days of us approving them, depending on your bank.

The 48-hour window helps us match a report to a vend while the log is fresh. It doesn't remove your statutory rights, and we'll still consider a late report where the circumstances justify it.

Change of mind

Buying from a machine is an immediate, in-person purchase, so the 14-day cooling-off period that applies to distance and online selling does not apply. Once an item has been dispensed we can't accept it back because you've changed your mind, or because you're unhappy with which cards were inside a sealed pack.

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, goods we supply must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If they aren't, you're entitled to a repair, replacement or refund as the Act provides. Nothing in these terms limits that.
