A4 · STOCK & SOURCING

Sealed product, printed prices, no games with the odds.

Every EdinCard slot holds a named, sealed Pokémon TCG item bought through legitimate trade supply. You can see what it is before you pay, and what you get is exactly what the label says.

SLOT TYPES

Three ways to fill a slot.

Every machine carries a mix across all three, so the same row never feels the same twice — and there's always something at an everyday price.

STANDARD SLOT

Single pack

One sealed booster from a current, in-print set. The everyday pull, and the slot we restock most often.

  • Current Mega Evolution-era sets
  • Restocked every service visit
  • Recent fills: Pitch Black, Chaos Rising, Perfect Order
  • The lowest price point in the machine
DOUBLE SLOT

Two-pack bundle

A mixed-era pairing dropped together, weighted toward sets that are getting harder to find on shelf.

  • One current pack, one older-set pack
  • Both packs listed on the slot label
  • Rotated as older stock runs out
  • Recent pairings: Destined Rivals, Black Bolt, Journey Together
VAULT SLOT

Limited fill

Special edition or reprint product, loaded in small runs. Shows up, sells out, doesn't come back on schedule.

  • Small-run and out-of-print product
  • One or two slots per machine, at most
  • Priced to what it costs us, not to hype
  • Past fills: Prismatic Evolutions, 151, Evolving Skies

SOURCING

Where the product actually comes from.

Stock is bought through UK trade distribution and established sealed-product wholesalers. Everything arrives sealed in case or box form and stays sealed until it's loaded into a slot.

Older and out-of-print product is bought as sealed, verified stock and inspected before loading. If a box shows any sign of tampering, resealing or damage, it doesn't go in a machine — it goes back.

We don't buy loose packs from secondary marketplaces, and we don't buy anything we can't trace to a supplier.

Our sealed-product guarantee

No pack in an EdinCard machine has ever been opened, weighed, mapped, x-rayed, sorted or resealed. Pull rates in our slots are the manufacturer's pull rates — nothing more, nothing less.

What we won't stock

No third-party "repacks", no custom mystery boxes, no graded-card gacha, no ungraded singles sold as chase items. If a product's appeal depends on you not knowing what's in it, it isn't going in the machine.

Price is on the slot

Printed on the label, charged at the reader. If those two ever disagree, the machine is wrong and you get refunded.

Stock rotates

Sets move as availability changes. A slot that's empty for two visits gets reassigned rather than left dark.

◎

Venues differ

The mix is tuned per site. A game shop and a student café don't want the same three rows, so they don't get them.

REQUESTS

Want something specific in a slot?

If there's a set you'd buy from a machine and we're not stocking it, tell us. We can't promise availability — some product simply isn't buyable at trade any more — but requests genuinely do shape what we chase down.

The same goes for venue hosts: if your regulars keep asking for one thing, that's the most useful stocking data we get.

ALSO WORTH KNOWING

  • Trading cards are not age-restricted product, so there's no age gate
  • Slots are single-item vends — there's no bundle-up or multi-buy discount
  • We don't buy, trade or take in cards at the machine
  • Condition of the sealed wrapper is checked at every service visit
  • Anything visibly damaged in a slot gets pulled, not sold at a discount
