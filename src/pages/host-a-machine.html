THE PITCH

A footfall draw that pays for its own corner.

A Pokémon machine isn't a snack machine. People come in specifically to use it, they bring friends, they open packs at the table, and they come back when new stock lands. For the right venue that's incremental footfall you weren't getting, attached to a revenue line you don't have to work for.

It's a particularly good fit if you already sell something people sit down with — coffee, pints, table time, tournaments — because the pack gets opened on your premises, not in the street.

VENUE QUESTIONS

The things venues ask first.

What does it cost us to host one?

Nothing up front and nothing ongoing. EdinCard funds the machine, the stock and the servicing. You take an agreed share of the revenue from every vend on your site, paid on a fixed schedule.

How big is the machine and what does it need?

Roughly the footprint of a small arcade cabinet — about a square metre of floor with clear access to the front. It needs a standard 13A socket within reach and an indoor position that can be secured overnight the same way the rest of your stock is.

Do our staff have to do anything?

No. They don't handle stock, cash, refunds or repairs. The only ask is that they let a customer with a problem know to email us, and let us in to service the unit during opening hours.

Who's liable if it breaks or someone's hurt?

The machine is our equipment, insured by us, and we carry public liability cover for it. Specific responsibilities are set out in the hosting agreement before install, so nobody's guessing after the fact.

How often is it restocked?

On a fixed schedule agreed with you at install, plus extra visits when a site is selling faster than expected. Every visit includes a refill, a slot-by-slot stock check and a mechanical test vend.

Can we get rid of it if it isn't working out?

Yes. The agreement is rolling with a short notice period on both sides. If the machine isn't earning its corner, we'd rather move it somewhere it will than argue with you about it.

We're outside Edinburgh — will you still come?

Edinburgh and the immediate surrounds are where we can service properly right now, and servicing is the whole business. Get in touch anyway: we're expanding, and knowing where the demand is decides where we go next.
