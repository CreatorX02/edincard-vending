A4 · WHAT'S INSIDE

Three ways to fill a slot.

Every machine carries a mix, so the same row never feels the same twice.

STANDARD SLOT

Single pack

One sealed booster from a current, in-print set. The everyday pull, and the slot we restock most often.

DOUBLE SLOT

Two-pack bundle

A mixed-era pairing dropped together, weighted toward sets that are getting harder to find on shelf.

VAULT SLOT

Limited fill

Special edition or reprint product, loaded in small runs. Shows up, sells out, doesn't come back on schedule.
