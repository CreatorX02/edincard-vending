A4 · WHAT'S INSIDE

Three ways to fill a slot.

Every machine carries a mix, so the same row never feels the same twice.

STANDARD SLOT

Single pack

One sealed booster from current, in-print sets. The everyday pull, restocked most often.

DOUBLE SLOT

Two pack bundle

A mixed-era pairing dropped together, weighted toward sets that are getting harder to find on shelf.

VAULT SLOT

Limited fill

Special edition or reprint product, loaded in small runs. Shows up, sells out, doesn't come back on schedule.

C3 · QUESTIONS

Before you tap in.

What's actually inside a slot?

Sealed, factory fresh Pokémon TCG packs. Nothing is opened, resealed, or swapped before it goes in the machine.

How do I pay?

Contactless card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay at the machine. No cash, no account, no app to install first.

What if a pack or slot misfires?

Email us within 48 hours with the machine location and time, and we'll sort a replacement or refund.

How do you decide what's stocked?

Rotated by set availability and demand at each venue, refilled on a set visiting schedule rather than left to run dry.

Do you take trade-ins or submissions?

Not at the machine, not yet. It's on the roadmap as we add more units.

Can I get a machine at my venue?

Yes. See the Host a machine section above, or email us directly.
